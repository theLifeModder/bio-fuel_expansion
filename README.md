## ABOUT _Tech-ES_ MOD PACK:

This Project is titled _Bio-Fuel Expansion_, and is the first in a series of add-ons that will make up the _Tech-ES_ (short for Technical Eco System) mod pack. The entire pack of add-ons will itself be released as one giant add-on when the project is complete.

The add-ons will all be stand-alone packs which add technical elements to the game and greatly expand it's content for the average technical player. It will add things such as new blocks and items, new tile entities (blocks that have their own inventories and interactions, such as hoppers), a custom enchantment system, and much more.

If you are a technical player (farm builder/designer, redstoner, etc.) like me and you feel like you've already done everything interesting and are finding it harder and harder to find projects that interest you (also like me), then check out this mod pack by all means! You are who it was designed for!

## ABOUT _Bio-Fuel Expansion_ ADD-ON:

#### General:

The _Bio-Fuel Expansion_ add-on is the first pack included in the Tech-ES line up. As of 10/1/2026, it is being rolled into it's production phase. It is ready for distribution, and so is available on CurseForge, MCPEDL, and various other mod distribution websites.

#### What The Mod Adds:

First of all, it adds two new items to the game. These are called "animal lard" and "bio-fuel". The details about these are as follows:

###### About Animal Lard:

Animal lard is the first item that you will want to acquire to get started with this add-on. You will need it to craft the bio-fuel to fuel your furnaces.

It is dropped by cows, sheep, pigs, and interestingly, striders, but only when their death is registered as a player kill, so farming them with kill methods like fall damage will not work. Of the 4 mobs that drop animal lard, striders are by far the best source. The first 3 (pigs, sheep, and cattle) have a 55% chance of dropping 1 piece of animal lard when killed by the player with a non-_Looting_ weapon. If killed with _Looting_, this chance increases by 15% for each level. So the chances are 55% for no _Looting_, 70% for _Looting I_, 85% for _Looting II_, and 100% for _Looting III_.

When an adult strider is killed via player kill, it has a 100% chance of dropping 1-2 pieces of animal lard. The _Looting_ adds an additional 0-1 drops per _Looting_ level. So a non-_Looting_ kill will yield 1-2 animal lard, _Looting I_ will yield 1-3, _Looting II_ will yield 1-4, and _Looting III_ will yield 1-5. So killing a strider without _Looting_ will give you the same or more lard than killing a cow, pig, or sheep with _Looting III_. This is why I say that farming striders will usually be your best bet for a lard farm.

Animal lard is both a food-source and a fuel-source, although it is itself not a very good source of either. Its main use is as an ingredient used to make bio-fuel, which is an excellent fuel-source.

It can also be enchanted if you have the _Bio-Fuel Expansion_ add-on's sister pack, the _Technical Enchantment_ addon, installed as well. If you are playing with the over-arching _Tech-ES_ mod pack activated, both should be present.

Currently, animal lard can contain 2 enchantments: "smeltmore" and "feedmore". Each of these has 3 levels, _smeltmore I_, _smeltmore II_, and _smeltmore III_, along with _feedmore I_, _feedmore II_, and _feedmore III_. Each of these adds value to the animal lard in a separate way.

_smeltmore_ adds value to animal lard as a furnace fuel, and _feedmore_ makes it a better food-source. As a fuel source, the unenchanted item is a very poor source indeed. One piece of unenchanted animal lard burns for only 2 seconds in a furnace, smoker, or blast furnace, and it takes 10 seconds to smelt 1 item in the furnace, so smelting each item takes a total of 5 pieces of animal lard. Smelting the same item in the smoker or blast furnace consumes 2.5 pieces of unenchanted animal lard. Enchanting with _smeltmore_ will up it's burn time by 1 second for each level, so animal lard with _smeltmore III_, for example' will burn for 5 seconds (2 seconds base time + 1 second X 3). When animal lard is crafted into bio-fuel, one piece will smelt 20 items, so that is by far your best option for maximum efficiency.

_Feedmore_, on the other hand, makes animal lard an excellent food-source. It makes it the best in the game, in fact. The regular item can be safely eaten, but it is not worth doing so, as it only gives you a half of a hunger bar and only has a saturation modifier of 0.3. So it only gives you a total of 1.6 hunger points. That is not even a whole hunger bar. Animal lard enchanted with _feedmore III_, however, has a nutrition value of 8 (4 hunger bars) and a saturation modifier of 0.875. The equation used to deturmine the given saturation of animal lard is 8(nutrition) X 0.875(saturation modifier) X 2 = 14. So the enchanted animal lard gives you 14 saturation. Coupled with the 8 nutrition points given, it makes for a total of 22 points, or 11 hunger bars per piece eaten! Though remember that 14 of the 22 are hidden and will not show up on your hunger bar. By comparison, a golden carrot gives you 20.4 and a cooked porkchop gives you 20.8. These are currently the two best food-sources in the vanilla game. So in summary, you'll definitely want to keep a stack of enchanted animal lard in your hot bar, as your main food-source, at all times.

###### About Bio-Fuel:

Producing bio-fuel to use in your furnaces is the main goal when playing this add-on. To get bio-fuel for your furnaces, you need to first acquire some animal lard, then you will need to put it together with a piece of bamboo and a piece of sugar cane in a crafting table. The recipe is shapeless, requires just 1 of each of the 3 items that are listed here, and makes exactly 1 jar of bio-fuel.

As a furnace fuel, bio-buel is excellent and enchanted bio-fuel is even better! One jar of bio-fuel will burn for 200 seconds. This will smelt exactly 20 items in a regular furnace and 40 in a blast furnace or smoker. In comparison, coal burns for 80 seconds (8 or 16 items), kepp blocks burn for 200 (20 or 40 items; the same as bio-fuel), and a lava bucket burns for a whopping 1000 (100 or 200 items). So even before enchanting, bio-fuel stands out asone of the best fuel-sources in the game. Enchanting with _smeltmore_, however, makes it even better. _Smeltmore I_ increases it's burn time to 300 seconds, _Smeltmore I_ ups it to 600 and _Smeltmore III_ to 900 seconds. So your best option is to create a fuel farm and have the output automatically enchanted via an auto-enchanter (a tile entity available in the _Technical Enchantment_ add-on that provides _smeltmore_ and _feedmore_), which then leads into either a storage system of some kind or else leads directly into some kind of furnace array.

Bio-fuel is edible, although it provides 0 nutrition and saturation. It also gives the player the _nausea_ effect for 10 seconds. _Feedmore_ is able to be added to this item, but it doesn't do anything. It just removes it's nausea effect.

#### Notes:

So the purpose of this mod is three-fold. It adds an efficient fuel-source, adds a great food-source (when coupled with the _Technical enchantment_ pack), and gives use a reason to farm a currently rarely-farmed mob: striders. To get the most out of this pack, I would definitely recommend using it in tandem with the _Technical Enchantment_ add-on. This adds a lot more features to an already well-sized feature set. While the _Technical Enchantment_ add-on has not been released as of the time of writing this, it is being rapidly developed, and so it should be available in the next few days.

## DOWNLOAD INSTRUCTIONS:

To download Bio-Fuel Expansion, just head over to [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/) in a browser, or open the [CurseForge-App](https://www.curseforge.com/download/app). Once on CurseForge, search "bio-fuel expansion" and the add-on should be listed in the results. Inside the listing for _Bio-Fuel Expansion_, there should be a button labeled _Download_. Click it. This will add the file containing the add-on, labeled _bio_fuel_expansion.mcaddon_ to your _Downloads_ folder.

## INSTALLATION INSTRUCTIONS:

Once downloaded, there will be a zipped folder named _bio_fuel_expansion.mcaddon_ in your _Downloads_ folder. You will need to double-click on it and choose to open it with Minecraft. This will automatically install the add-on to Minecraft and open the game. Once in gane, select to edit the world you want the add-on on. In the menu on the left side of the screen, select _behavior packs_. This will give you a list of available packs. In this list, select _bio-fuel expansion_ and click _activate_. That's it! Your add-on is now active!
